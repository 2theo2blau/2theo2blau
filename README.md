# Hi, I'm Theo 👋

I like figuring out how things work by taking them apart and putting them back together again. I've been building computers since I was 12 years old, and they've been a fixture in my life ever since. 

My background is in economics and finance, but I approach my work from a lot of directions. Before finance I was a pastry cook in Michelin-starred restaurants, where I learned the discipline and attention to detail I bring to everything I do.

These days, I work mostly in the domains of finance, signal processing, machine learning and networking/system administration. I've been running a homelab for the last few years, which is home to many of my projects and experiments.

I work remotely as an analyst at a private equity secondaries fund in London, where my duties cover research, due diligence, financial modeling and some automation and technical infrastructure work.

I also volunteer with Quantum Village at security conferences like DEF CON and BSides. In addition to helping organize and manage the village at conferences, I have also helped with testing and demonstrating open-source quantum devices -- such as an [NV Diamond Magnetometer](https://github.com/QuantumVillage/UncutGem) and an [Optical Phase Diffusion Quantum RNG](https://github.com/QuantumVillage/EntropyLoop).

#### Some of the personal projects I am most proud of:

 - An [implementation of the "Diffolio" paper](https://github.com/2theo2blau/diffolio) which uses denoising diffision probabilistic models to optimize portfolio allocations for any basket of stocks or assets, taking into account market conditions, without traditional mean-variance methods.

 - My [scripts for software defined radio](https://github.com/2theo2blau/sdr-experiments), which covers several experiments I've run with a HackRF -- including watching the entire FRS/GMRS band at once, with realtime burst detection and a classifier that uses statistical inference to ensure only voice transmissions are written to disk. Additionally, it also includes a module for receiving and decoding digital (DMR) voice transmissions using mbelib and dsd-fme.

 - My build of the [Stanford Pupper v3](https://github.com/2theo2blau/pupperv3-mjx) for which I built a quadruped robot (robot dog) from scratch, including hardware assembly, training locomotion policies using reinforcement learning in MuJoCo/JAX, and managing sim2real transfer.

 - My fork of Quantum Village's [Entropy Loop](https://github.com/2theo2blau/EntropyLoop-DMA), which implements higher throughput firmware using direct memory access (DMA) on the microcontroller and streams the raw entropy over a USB link rather than just its hash.
